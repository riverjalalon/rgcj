06Process

Pick the goal first. Then the metric, the baseline and the change.

Clear stages and clear approvals, so you always know where your content stands. Every month ends with a report like the ones on this page.

01Audit & baselineExport Insights, review the profile and competitors.
02Pillars & voiceWhat the brand posts about and how it sounds.
03Plan the gridPillars and formats on the calendar and the 3 × 3.
04Design & writeArt direction, captions, scheduling, replies.
05Report & repeatTurn the winners into series.

Goal → metric

Likes and raw views are context, never the verdict
GoalWhat I readPrimary
Awareness
Did new people see it, and stay?		Reach, non-follower reach %, 3-sec hold, watch timeWatch time
Consideration
Did they value it?		Saves and sends per 1K reach, profile visits → followsSaves + sends
Conversion
Did they act?		Link clicks, UTM sessions, sign-ups, salesQualified action
How I set the baseline
  • Same account, metric and window length. A 27-day window is never compared with a 5-month one as if they were equal.
  • The benchmark is the median of the last 10 posts in the same format, so one viral post can't skew it.
  • Rates, not totals: saves per 1K reach, follows per 1K impressions.
  • Single posts read at 7 days. Cadence or format changes get 4–6 weeks.
IfStrong 3-sec hold, weak watch timeTHENcut tighter and move the payoff earlier.
IfHigh reach, few saves or sendsTHENadd a checklist slide or a send-to-a-friend prompt.
IfLoyal engagement, slow followsTHENa sharper hook and a "why follow" line.
IfPeople watch but don't respondTHENpolls and question stickers, re-read in 2 weeks.
